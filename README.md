## 💡 Featured Work

### 🎵 Splash Music — From Hum to Hit

> *"We're not a music company, we allow people to make their own music. You don't need industry connections — just your imagination can get your music to millions."*

Building the future of music creation with AI. Our HummingLM model transforms human humming into fully produced tracks, democratizing music production for everyone.

**Tech Stack:** AWS Trainium • SageMaker HyperPod • 2PB Data Pipeline • Multi-region deployment

<details>
<summary><strong>📊 Technical Achievements</strong></summary>

- **54% cost reduction** migrating from H100 to AWS Trainium
- **159% efficiency improvement** in model training
- **2x faster training iterations** with equivalent accuracy
- Scaled from prototype to **10M+ concurrent users**
- Built distributed teams across **6 global offices**

</details>

### 🎮 Twitch — Soundtrack Engineering

Led the engineering team that built **Soundtrack by Twitch**, a fully rights-cleared music library with multitrack audio separation. Solved the DMCA challenge for millions of streamers.

**Impact:** 70K+ RPS • Real-time ML operations • Live-to-VOD automation

### 📚 Audible — Creator Marketplace

Launched international creator marketplace expansion, supporting millions of narrators, publishers, and content providers worldwide.
