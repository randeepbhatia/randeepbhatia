## 💡 Featured Case Study: HummingLM on AWS Trainium

> *"With SageMaker HyperPod and Trainium, our researchers experiment as fast as our community creates. We're not just keeping up with music trends—we're setting them."*

**The Challenge:** Train a 10B parameter music generation model cost-effectively.

**The Solution:** Migrated from H100 GPUs to AWS Trainium with SageMaker HyperPod.

**Results:**
- ✅ **54% cost reduction** vs NVIDIA H100
- ✅ **159% efficiency improvement** in training
- ✅ **2x faster training iterations** with equivalent accuracy
- ✅ **2PB data migration** to Amazon S3
